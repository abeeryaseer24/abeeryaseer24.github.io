Independent research, sole author  |  github.com/abeeryaseer24/Power-Systems
  • Built an open simulation platform of 17 real transmission grids rebuilt from OpenStreetMap, with AC power flow, protection and frequency control, and evaluated every model only on data it had never seen.
  • Demand forecasting (Great Britain): a gradient-boosted model backtested over 46,478 half-hours reached 3.52% MAPE; averaged with the system operator's forecast it reached 2.64%, better than either alone.
  • Learned AC power flow: caught 96–99.6% of overloads on withheld N-1 outages, 200–770× faster than a fast decoupled load flow.

Will There Be Load Shedding Tonight? Forecasting Evening-Peak Load Shedding in Bangladesh Against the Operator's Own Plan2026, draft
Independent research, sole author
  • Built an 11.5-year daily series (April 2015 to November 2026, 3,607 days) by joining PGCB's hourly record with NLDC's daily reports.
  • Averaging gradient-boosted trees with persistence cut the error by 10% for tonight and 14% for tomorrow; on 508 test days the model's error was 220 MW against 257 MW for NLDC's published plan.
  • Deployed as a live predictor that updates from each new NLDC report.
